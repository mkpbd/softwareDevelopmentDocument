What Are the Types of Liquidity ?
1. Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL) (Resistance or supply zone )
2. Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL) (support or demand Zone)
3. Internal Liquidity
4. External Liquidity

Each type of liquidity plays a unique role in how the market moves, and recognizing
these zones is vital for effective trade execution.

#### Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL)
Buy-side liquidity refers to a cluster of buy-stop orders located above key market levels.
These stops are typically placed by traders who are short, acting

2. Once price reaches these levels, stop orders are triggered
