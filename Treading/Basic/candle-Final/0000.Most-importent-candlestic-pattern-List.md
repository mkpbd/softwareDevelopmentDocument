# ⚠️ Reality Check (Important)

Not all 100 patterns are equally useful.

👉 **Focus on these 10–15 first:**

- Engulfing (Bullish & Bearish)
- Hammer / Shooting Star
- Doji
- Morning Star / Evening Star
- Three White Soldiers / Black Crows
- Harami
- Pin Bar
- Inside Bar
- Break of Structure

These are what most professional traders actually rely on.
