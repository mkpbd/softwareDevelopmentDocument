1. Overview

SMC Master Pro v15 is a Pine Script v5 indicator implementing the complete ICT (Inner Circle Trader) and Smart Money Concepts methodology with institutional-grade execution logic. It detects Break of Structure (BOS), Change of Character (CHoCH), Order Blocks, Mitigation Blocks, Breaker Blocks, Fair Value Gaps, Inverted FVGs, BPRs, liquidity sweeps and grabs, AMD setups, Turtle Soup patterns, HTF POIs, OTE zones, and full session/kill-zone context — synthesised through a weighted confluence engine with mandatory hard-gate filters.

Design philosophy

  • Bar-close confirmation — Every structural event is gated by barstate.isconfirmed to eliminate repaint.
  • ATR-relative thresholds — All distance / size / displacement filters scale with volatility; the same parameters work across forex, futures, crypto, and equities.
  • Frozen reference zones — Premium / Discount, Fibonacci, and POI confluence anchor to the leg that fired the last CHoCH, preventing mid-trend drift.
  • Quality over quantity — Multi-layer filtering (confluence score + hard gate + cooldown + daily R cap + max-per-KZ + consecutive loss cutoff).
  • Realistic backtest — Default fill at next-bar-open removes signal-bar lookahead; same-bar TP/SL ambiguity resolves via tick-approximation.

Target audience

  • Scalpers operating 1M / 3M / 5M timeframes
  • Intraday traders following ICT methodology
  • Discretionary traders wanting structured confluence checks
  • Algo developers studying institutional structure
Important. This indicator is an analysis tool, not a fully automated trading system. The position tracker visualises trade outcomes for backtesting but does not place orders. Use signals as confirmation alongside discretionary judgment.

4. Core SMC / ICT Concepts

For readers new to ICT / SMC, here is a self-contained primer of the concepts the indicator detects.

4.1 BOS — Break of Structure

A continuation signal. In an uptrend, price closes above the most-recent swing high (HH). Confirms institutional momentum continuing in the same direction. Bearish BOS = close below the most-recent swing low (LL) in a downtrend.

4.2 CHoCH — Change of Character

A reversal signal. The first counter-trend break of structure. In a downtrend, price closes above the most-recent lower-high (LH) — the trend's character has changed; a new uptrend may begin. CHoCH precedes BOS.

4.3 MSS — Market Structure Shift

A micro-CHoCH on internal structure (shorter pivots). Used as a trigger for entry timing. The indicator's intSwingLen=3 pivots produce MSS events while swingLen=8 pivots produce HTF context.

4.4 Order Block (OB)

The last opposite-color candle before an impulsive move that breaks structure. A bullish OB is the last bearish candle before a strong bull impulse. Price retraces back into the OB to "fill the order" of institutions that pushed price away.

4.5 Mitigation Block (MB)

An Order Block that forms immediately after a CHoCH. Stronger than a normal OB because it represents institutional commitment to the new trend direction. Indicator tags these in purple within mbRecencyBars (default 10) of a CHoCH.

4.6 Breaker Block

A mitigated OB that subsequently gets closed THROUGH (price violates the opposite side). The zone inverts: a former bullish OB closed-through-below becomes bearish resistance.

4.7 FVG — Fair Value Gap

A 3-candle pattern where the middle candle's range creates a price gap that the wicks of candle 1 and candle 3 don't fill. Bullish FVG: low[0] > high[2]. The gap is "fair value" that price typically returns to before continuing.

4.8 iFVG — Inversion FVG

An FVG that fully fills and closes through. Like a Breaker but for gaps. Bull FVG → fully filled → close below bottom → becomes bearish resistance zone.

4.9 BPR — Balanced Price Range

Overlap zone where a bullish FVG and bearish FVG share price area. Strong reaction zone.

4.10 Liquidity Sweep vs Grab

TypeBehaviourInterpretation
SweepWick takes liquidity beyond swing, then closes back insideReversal signal — stop hunt complete, expect bounce
GrabPrice breaks through swing AND closes throughContinuation signal — momentum continues in break direction

4.11 PD — Premium / Discount Zones

Range divided at 50% (equilibrium). Buy in Discount (lower half). Sell in Premium (upper half). Frozen at most-recent CHoCH to prevent mid-trend drift.

4.12 OTE — Optimal Trade Entry

The 0.618 – 0.786 Fibonacci retracement zone of the most-recent CHoCH leg. ICT's high-probability entry band.

4.13 AMD — Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution

Power of 3 daily cycle. Asia accumulates a range, London manipulates (sweeps Asia H or L), NY distributes (trends). Indicator marks when London wicks Asia high/low and reverses.

4.14 Kill Zones

High-probability trading windows: London Open (02:00-05:00 NY), NY AM (08:30-11:00 NY), Silver Bullet (10:00-11:00 NY), NY PM (13:30-16:00 NY).

4.15 Turtle Soup

N-bar false break. Price wicks above the N-bar high (or below N-bar low) but closes back inside. Classic stop-run setup.

5. Market Structure Engine

5.1 Dual-pivot architecture (v15 P0-1)

v15 separates structure detection into two parallel tracks:

TrackPivot lengthDrives
PRIMARYintSwingLen (default 3)internalTrend, BOS / CHoCH labels, entry MSS, PD freeze, sweep / grab detection
HTF CONTEXTswingLen (default 8)Trendlines, swing-based SL fallback

Rationale: the v14 design used swingLen=8 as the trend driver, meaning structure was confirmed 8 bars after the fact and signals fired on retroactively-known levels. v15 uses 3-bar pivots for the trend logic (confirmed 3 bars later, much faster) and keeps 8-bar pivots only for HTF visual context.

5.2 Pivot retro-scan

When a pivot forms at bar_index - intSwingLen, the bars between formation and now (excluding the pivot bar itself) are re-scanned. If any intervening close (or wick when useWickForBOS is on) already broke the new pivot level, that level is marked lastSHBroken=true immediately. Prevents a "phantom" intact swing from being used by downstream logic.

5.3 BOS / CHoCH classification

breakAbove = isconfirmed AND lastSH non-na AND not lastSHBroken AND
    (useWickForBOS ? high : close) > lastSH AND not sameBarBlockSH

bosBull = breakAbove AND internalTrend == 1
chochBull = breakAbove AND (internalTrend == -1 OR (firstShiftAsCHoCH AND internalTrend == 0))
initBull = breakAbove AND not firstShiftAsCHoCH AND internalTrend == 0

5.4 Dual-break resolution

If a single confirmed bar breaks both lastSH AND lastSL simultaneously (gap candle), the indicator resolves the ambiguity via ATR-relative dominance:

bullDominance = (high - lastSH) / atr
bearDominance = (lastSL - low) / atr

If bullDominance > bearDominance → keep bull break, drop bear
If bearDominance > bullDominance → keep bear break, drop bull
If equal → follow existing internalTrend direction

5.5 Internal MSS gate

When useInternalMSS=true, every entry must coincide with either:

  • An MSS event in the trade direction on the current bar (BOS / CHoCH / INIT), OR
  • An existing internalTrend already pointing in the trade direction.

Counter-trend trades require explicit CHoCH confirmation.

6. POI Detection

6.1 Order Block (OB) detection

An OB is created when:

  1. A strong impulse candle appears (body ≥ obStrength × range AND range ≥ obImpulseATR × ATR), AND
  2. An opposite-color candle exists within obLookback bars before it (1–8 bars), AND
  3. (Strict mode) The impulse bar must coincide with a BOS / CHoCH / INIT event when obRequireBOS=true.

The OB bounds = the prior opposite-color candle's high and low.

6.2 OB mitigation modes

ModeTriggerUse case
TouchWick enters the zoneAggressive — first reaction zone
50% fill (default)Wick reaches midpointICT canonical — consequent encroachment
Full fillPrice reaches opposite extremeConservative — full retest

6.3 MB (Mitigation Block) tagging

OBs that form within mbRecencyBars (default 10) of the last CHoCH are tagged as MB and rendered in purple. They represent institutional continuation in the new direction.

6.4 Breaker Block promotion (two-phase)

The Breaker Block promotion is intentionally two-phase to enforce ICT correctness:

  1. Phase 1 — Mitigation: Mitigated OB is added to a "pending breaker" tracker with its top/bottom and creation bar.
  2. Phase 2 — Close-through: On a subsequent confirmed close through the zone (close below bull OB bottom, or close above bear OB top), the pending OB is promoted to a Breaker.
  3. If close-through doesn't happen within breakerPendingAge bars (default 30), the pending entry is dropped.

6.5 FVG detection

bullFVG = low[0] > high[2] AND close[1] > open[1] AND
    fvgBullSizeOK AND fvgDisplacementOK

bearFVG = high[0] < low[2] AND close[1] < open[1] AND
    fvgBearSizeOK AND fvgDisplacementOK

fvgDisplacementOK = (high[1] - low[1]) ≥ atr × fvgDisplacementATR

Gap size must be ≥ fvgMinSizeAtr × ATR (default 0.10). Middle candle range must be ≥ fvgDisplacementATR × ATR (default 1.5) — filters trivial gaps.

6.6 iFVG promotion (two-phase)

  1. FVG reaches mitigation threshold (Touch / 50% / Full per fvgMitigationMode) → added to pending-inversion tracker.
  2. Subsequent confirmed close-through (close below bottom for bull FVG, close above top for bear FVG) within fvgPendingAge bars → promoted to iFVG.
  3. iFVGs age out after ifvgMaxAge bars (default 60).

7. HTF POI & Multi-Timeframe Bias

7.1 HTF FVG / OB detection

A single merged request.security call to htfPOITF (default 240) retrieves 11 series — high[1], low[1], high[2], low[2], high[3], low[3], close[1], open[1], close[2], open[2], ATR[1] — all on closed HTF bars.

HTF Bull FVG: htfLow1 > htfHigh3 AND middle candle is bullish.
HTF Bear FVG: htfHigh1 < htfLow3 AND middle candle is bearish.

HTF OB: impulse candle ([1]) range ≥ HTF ATR (strong); prior candle ([2]) is opposite color — [2]'s H/L bounds the OB.

7.2 MTF Trend bias

For each HTF (htf1=60, htf2=240, htf3=D):
  trend = closed-bar EMA20 > closed-bar EMA50 ? +1 : EMA20 < EMA50 ? -1 : 0

mtfBullBias = trendHTF1==1 AND trendHTF2==1 AND trendHTF3==1
mtfBearBias = trendHTF1==-1 AND trendHTF2==-1 AND trendHTF3==-1

Note v14 B1 fix preserved: EMAs are read with [1] shift on closed HTF bars — no live-bar fallback, no HTF rollover repaint.

7.3 HTF alignment hard gate

When hgRequireHTF=true, entries require ≥2 of 3 HTF EMAs aligned with trade direction.

8. Liquidity, Sweeps & Grabs

8.1 Equal H/L detection

Two consecutive swings within tolerance form a liquidity level. Tolerance modes:

  • ATR fraction (default): |lastSH - prevSH| ≤ atr × liqToleranceAtr (0.3 default = 30% of ATR)
  • Percent: |a-b|/a × 100 < liqTolerance

8.2 Sweep — wick + reverse

bullSweep = isconfirmed AND detectSweeps AND swingSignificant AND
    not lastSLBroken AND low < lastSL AND close > lastSL AND
    (close > open OR close > close[1])

swingSignificant = (lastSH - lastSL) ≥ atr × sweepMinRange. Default 0.5×ATR — filters trivial micro-sweeps.

8.3 Grab — wick + continuation (v15 P0-2)

bullLiqGrab = isconfirmed AND detectLiqGrabs AND swingSignificant AND
    not lastSLBroken AND low < lastSL AND close < lastSL AND close < open

Bull SL grab = bearish continuation (institutions took bull stops and continued lower).
Bear SH grab = bullish continuation.

8.4 De-duplication

One sweep label per swing index — prevents stacked labels on consecutive bars taking out the same swing. Grabs use a separate dedup tracker.

8.5 AMD detection

  1. Asia session: build high/low.
  2. Asia close: snapshot range if ≥ amdMinRangeATR × ATR.
  3. London session: detect wick of Asia high (bearish AMD setup) or Asia low (bullish AMD setup) that closes back inside.
  4. One AMD signal per day (amdSweptToday flag).

9. Premium / Discount Zones & Fibonacci

9.1 Frozen PD reference

On every CHoCH / INIT event, the indicator snapshots:

  • pdRefHigh = lastSH at the moment of CHoCH
  • pdRefLow = lastSL at the moment of CHoCH
  • pdRefBar = current bar index

These freeze until the next CHoCH. PD box, equilibrium line, and zone-gate filter all use these frozen values — preventing mid-trend drift as new pivots form.

9.2 PD zone gate

When hgRequireZone=true:

  • Longs only enter when close < _zoneMid (in Discount)
  • Shorts only enter when close > _zoneMid (in Premium)

9.3 Fibonacci anchor (v15 P2-10)

ModeAnchor
CHoCH leg (frozen) — defaultUses pdRefHigh/pdRefLow from the last CHoCH. Matches displayed PD box.
Last swings (legacy)Uses current lastSH/lastSL — v14 behaviour.

9.4 OTE Zone

0.618 – 0.786 retracement band of the anchored swing, shaded gold. ICT's optimal entry band.

11. Candle & Chart Patterns

11.1 Engulfing

bullEngulf = isBullC AND isBearC[1] AND open ≤ close[1] AND close ≥ open[1] AND
    (close - open) > (open[1] - close[1])

11.2 Pin Bar

bullPin = body ≥ atr × pinMinBodyAtr (default 0.05) AND
    lowerWick ≥ body × pinBarRatio (1.8) AND
    lowerWick ≥ range × 0.55 AND
    upperWick ≤ body × 0.5 AND close > open

11.3 Double Top / Bottom

Two consecutive swing extremes within atr × 0.3 of each other, within swingLen + 5 bars, with an intervening swing on the opposite side.

11.4 Turtle Soup

nBarHi = ta.highest(high[1], turtleSoupLookback) // 20-bar default
turtleSoupBear = high > nBarHi AND close < nBarHi AND close < open

14. Signal Generation Pipeline

The complete entry pipeline, in evaluation order:

  1. Trigger: bullish engulfing OR (pin AND signals-enabled) OR (sweep AND signals-enabled)
  2. Displacement gate (v15 P1-5): engulf requires body ≥ dispBodyAtr × ATR; pins exempt by default
  3. MSS gate: intMssBull OR intTrend==1
  4. Confluence threshold: bullScore ≥ minConfluence
  5. KZ gate (optional): not killzoneOnly OR inAnyKZ
  6. Cooldown: bars since last signal ≥ signalCooldownBars
  7. Bar-close gate: barstate.isconfirmed
  8. Hard gate: HTF aligned AND zone OK AND KZ-hard OK AND liq OK AND vol healthy
  9. News blocker: not in news window
  10. Daily R cap: dayRealizedR > dailyLossR
  11. KZ trade cap: trades this KZ < max
  12. Consec loss cap: consecLosses < maxConsecLosses

All twelve must pass. Failure of any → no signal.

16. TP/SL Exit Engine

16.1 Same-bar ambiguity resolution (v15 P1-4)

When a single bar's range contains both SL and TP, which counts first?

ModeDecision rule
Tick-approx by candle open (default)Compare |open - SL| vs |open - TP|. The closer one is touched first.
SL-first (pessimistic, legacy v14)Always assume SL first.
TP-first (optimistic)Always assume TP first.

Tick-approx provides a more realistic estimate than v14's blanket SL-first assumption while remaining deterministic without per-tick data.

16.2 R tracking & consecutive loss counter

On every exit (SL or TP3):

tradeR = totalRealized / |entry - origSL|
dayRealizedR += tradeR

If tradeR < 0: consecLosses += 1
Else: consecLosses := 0

16.3 Fill mode (v15 P1-7)

ModeBehaviour
Next bar open (default, realistic)Entry/flip signal sets a pending flag; the next bar's open is used as posEntry.
Signal close (legacy)Entry filled at the signal bar's close — optimistic, equivalent to lookahead.

19. Troubleshooting

RE10045 — array.get out of bounds

Fixed in v15. If still seen, ensure the file is the latest commit. The fix is in f_priceInBoxes: guard with nested if instead of relying on short-circuit AND.

"No signals firing"

  • Check Hard Gate stacking — five filters all required by default. Disable individually to isolate.
  • requireConfluence=false bypasses score check.
  • Range filter may be flagging chop. Try useRangeFilter=false.
  • Daily R cap may be hit (red dashboard guard row).
  • Consecutive loss cap may be hit.

"Too many signals"

  • Raise minConfluence (default 8 → try 10–12).
  • Enable hgRequireKZ=true — restrict to kill zones.
  • Raise signalCooldownBars.
  • Lower maxTradesPerKZ.

"Signals appear/disappear during forming bar"

Should not happen in v15 with waitForBarClose=true (default). If observed, check that toggle is on.

"Backtest results don't match live"

  • Ensure fillMode="Next bar open" (default in v15).
  • Use ambigMode="Tick-approx" for realistic same-bar resolution.
  • Live broker spreads/slippage not modelled — expect 5–15% degradation vs backtest.

"Drawing limit reached" warnings

Max boxes/lines/labels = 500. Indicator manages bounded arrays but heavy charts (high TF + long history + all toggles ON) may saturate. Switch to "Minimal" or "Trading" preset, or reduce maxFVGs, obMaxBoxes, etc.
