The best pairs for these timeframes are **EUR/USD**, **GBP/USD**, and **USD/JPY** due to their deep liquidity and minimal "slippage."

## 1-Minute Strategy: The EMA Crossover + Momentum Filter

This is a high-frequency trend-following setup designed to capture quick 5-8 pip moves.

### 1. Indicators Setup

- **EMA 9 (Exponential Moving Average):** Your fast signal line.
- **EMA 21:** Your baseline trend.
- **RSI (Period 14):** Used to confirm momentum.

### 2. Entry Rules

- **Long Entry:**
    
    1. The **9 EMA** crosses above the **21 EMA**.
    2. The price is trading above both EMAs.
    3. **RSI** is above 50 (showing bullish momentum).

- **Short Entry:**
    
    1. The **9 EMA** crosses below the **21 EMA**.
    2. The price is trading below both EMAs.
    3. **RSI** is below 50.

### 3. Exit Strategy

- **Stop Loss:** 3-5 pips (usually behind the most recent swing high/low).
- **Take Profit:** 8-12 pips, or exit when the EMAs cross back in the opposite direction.

## 5-Minute Strategy: Bollinger Band Mean Reversion

On the 5-minute chart, "market noise" is slightly reduced, making mean reversion strategies highly effective, especially during the London or New York sessions.

### 1. Indicators Setup

- **Bollinger Bands (20, 2):** To identify overextended prices.
- **Stochastic Oscillator (5, 3, 3):** To find overbought/oversold turns.

### 2. Entry Rules

- **Short (Sell) Setup:**
    
    1. Price touches or pierces the **Upper Bollinger Band**.
    2. The **Stochastic** lines cross downward above the 80 level.
    3. Wait for a bearish candle to close back inside the band.
- **Long (Buy) Setup:**
    
    1. Price touches or pierces the **Lower Bollinger Band**.
    2. The **Stochastic** lines cross upward below the 20 level.
    3. Wait for a bullish candle to close back inside the band.
