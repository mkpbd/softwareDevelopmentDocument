### অটোমেশন করার সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ ফিচার লিস্ট (Priority Wise)

**Tier-1 (অবশ্যই করতে হবে)**

- Attendance → Auto Late/Early/Absent calculation
- Leave → Auto balance update + approval workflow
- Payroll → Full auto calculation (Basic, House Rent, Medical, Overtime, Bonus, Deduction)
- Payroll → Auto journal voucher in Accounting
- Bank Reconciliation (Auto matching)
- Tax calculation & e-TIN, e-Return ready report
- Offer Letter, Appointment Letter, Salary Slip, Experience Letter (auto generate)

**Tier-2 (খুব ভালো হবে)**

- Recruitment → Auto email + SMS to candidates
- Employee Onboarding → Auto email credentials + document request
- Performance → Auto reminder + score calculation
- Loan → Auto EMI deduction from salary
- Budget vs Actual report (Department wise)

**Tier-3 (Advanced Automation)**

- AI based candidate shortlisting
- Predictive attrition analysis
- Auto salary increment suggestion based on performance
- Chatbot for employees (leave balance, payslip, loan status)
